Privacy

Last updated: 25 November 2026

Short version

We do not collect personal data. Our pages use no cookies, no trackers and no sign-up. Our AI-chat plugins and skills send nothing about you or your business to us.

Websites

eupriceindex.com, coffee.eupriceindex.com and their index files (index.json) are static pages. We do not run analytics scripts and do not set cookies.

AI-chat plugins and skills

  • The plugins "EU Price Index: Coffee" and "EU Price Index: Chocolate" (Claude) and the copy-paste instructions for other AI chats only download one public file: https://coffee.eupriceindex.com/index.json or https://eupriceindex.com/chocolate/index.json.
  • The address has ?src=skill so we can count, in total, how many downloads come from AI chats. No other parameter is sent.
  • Your question, your prices, your name, email or shop name are never sent to us. The comparison happens inside your AI chat.
  • Your AI chat provider (for example Anthropic) processes your conversation under its own privacy policy.

Hosting

The files are delivered by GitHub Pages and Cloudflare. Like any web server, they process technical request data (IP address, time, requested address) to deliver the file, under their own privacy policies. We do not receive individual request logs; we may look at total request counts only.

Retention

We store no personal data, so there is nothing to keep or delete. Aggregate counts (numbers only) may be kept indefinitely.
