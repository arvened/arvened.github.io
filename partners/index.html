Partner with us

Reach shoppers who are comparing prices — right when they compare.

EU Price Index publishes independent, regularly updated price data for everyday treats — chocolate first, then coffee, tea, cheese, cosmetics and pet food — across Poland, Germany, Czechia, Belgium and France.

What we offer

  • Sponsored store card — a highlighted card for your shop on the index page of your category, clearly labelled "Sponsored".
  • Sponsored article — a guide or review on our site, clearly labelled "Sponsored", with a link to your store.

What sponsorship does not do

Sponsors never change our numbers. Prices, rankings and price history are calculated the same way for every store, sponsor or not. See our Editorial policy.

How to start

Email partners@eupriceindex.com with:

  1. Your store or brand and website
  2. Countries you sell to
  3. What you are interested in (store card / article)
  4. Your budget and preferred dates

We reply within 2 business days. Invoices are issued in EUR.
