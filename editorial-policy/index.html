Editorial policy

Last updated: 7 November 2026

How we collect prices

We collect publicly listed prices from online stores in the EU on a regular schedule. Every price shows the date it was checked.

How the index is calculated

The same method applies to every store. We do not accept payment to include, exclude, move or change any price or ranking.

Sponsored content

  • Sponsored placements are always labelled "Sponsored".
  • Links in sponsored content are marked rel="sponsored".
  • Sponsors do not see or approve our price data or rankings before publication.
  • We may decline any sponsorship that does not fit our readers.

AI use

We use automation and AI tools to collect data and draft texts. A person reviews the method and the published results.

Corrections

Found a wrong price? Email partners@eupriceindex.com with the link. We fix confirmed errors.

Who we are

EU Price Index is run by Eduard Arbitman, Kyiv, Ukraine. Contact: partners@eupriceindex.com.
